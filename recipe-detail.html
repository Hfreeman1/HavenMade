Honey-Glazed Salmon Bowl

Tender salmon coated in a sweet soy-honey glaze, served with fluffy rice and fresh seasoned cucumbers.

⏱ About 30 min 🍽 2 servings

What you'll need ♡

  • 2 salmon fillets (about 4–5 oz each)
  • 1 cup uncooked jasmine rice
  • 1 cucumber, thinly sliced
  • 2 tablespoons honey
  • 2 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 1 teaspoon minced garlic
  • 1 teaspoon oil
  • 1 teaspoon rice vinegar
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste

Let's get cooking!

  1. Cook jasmine rice according to its package directions.
  2. Mix honey, soy sauce, and garlic in a small bowl.
  3. Pat salmon dry, season lightly with pepper, and brush with oil.
  4. Air-fry at 390°F for about 7–10 minutes, depending on thickness, until the salmon reaches 145°F internally.
  5. Warm the honey-soy mixture in a small saucepan until bubbling, then spoon it over the cooked salmon.
  6. Toss cucumber slices with rice vinegar and a tiny pinch of salt.
  7. Serve the salmon over rice with cucumbers on the side.

Chicken Noodle Soup

A warm, comforting bowl filled with tender chicken, noodles, and veggies.

⏱ About 45 min 🍽 4 servings

What you'll need ♡

  • 1 lb boneless chicken breast
  • 6 cups chicken broth
  • 2 carrots, chopped
  • 2 celery stalks, chopped
  • 2 cups dry egg noodles
  • 2 teaspoons minced garlic
  • 1 tablespoon butter
  • 1 teaspoon dried parsley
  • Salt and pepper, to taste

Let's get cooking!

  1. Melt butter in a large pot over medium heat.
  2. Add carrots and celery. Cook for about 5 minutes.
  3. Add garlic and stir for 30 seconds.
  4. Pour in broth and add chicken, parsley, salt, and pepper.
  5. Simmer gently until chicken reaches 165°F internally, about 15–20 minutes depending on thickness.
  6. Remove chicken, shred with two forks, and return it to the pot.
  7. Add noodles and cook according to the package directions until tender.
  8. Taste, adjust seasoning, and enjoy warm.
Easy Dinner

BBQ Chicken Flatbread Pizza

Sweet and smoky BBQ chicken, melty cheese, and crispy flatbread.

⏱ About 20 min 🍽 2 servings

What you'll need ♡

  • 2 flatbreads or naan breads
  • 1 cup cooked shredded chicken
  • 1/3 cup BBQ sauce
  • 1 cup shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1/4 cup shredded cheddar cheese
  • 1 teaspoon olive oil
  • Optional: chopped parsley

Let's get cooking!

  1. Preheat oven to 425°F.
  2. Place flatbreads on a baking sheet and brush edges lightly with oil.
  3. Spread BBQ sauce over each flatbread.
  4. Add fully cooked shredded chicken and both cheeses.
  5. Bake for about 10–12 minutes, until the cheese melts and edges are golden.
  6. Let cool briefly, sprinkle with parsley if desired, and slice.

Tomato Soup & Grilled Cheese

Creamy tomato soup with golden, buttery grilled cheese for dipping.

⏱ About 25 min 🍽 2 servings

What you'll need ♡

  • 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
  • 1 cup vegetable or chicken broth
  • 1/4 cup heavy cream
  • 1 tablespoon butter, plus more for sandwiches
  • 1 teaspoon minced garlic
  • 1/2 teaspoon dried basil
  • 4 slices sandwich bread
  • 4 slices cheddar cheese
  • Salt and pepper, to taste

Let's get cooking!

  1. Melt 1 tablespoon butter in a pot over medium-low heat.
  2. Add garlic and cook briefly until fragrant.
  3. Stir in crushed tomatoes, broth, and basil.
  4. Simmer for 15 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  5. Stir in heavy cream and season to taste. Keep warm.
  6. Butter the outside of each sandwich, with cheese between the bread slices.
  7. Cook sandwiches in a skillet over medium-low heat until golden on both sides and the cheese melts.
  8. Slice sandwiches and serve with soup.
One-Pot Favorite

Homestyle Chili

A hearty, comforting chili made with ground turkey, beans, and cozy seasonings.

⏱ About 40 min 🍽 4 servings

What you'll need ♡

  • 1 lb ground turkey
  • 1 can (15 oz) kidney beans, drained
  • 1 can (15 oz) diced tomatoes
  • 1 can (8 oz) tomato sauce
  • 1/2 cup chicken broth
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 tablespoon mild chili powder
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • Salt and pepper, to taste

Let's get cooking!

  1. Heat oil in a large pot over medium heat.
  2. Add ground turkey and cook, breaking it apart, until fully cooked to 165°F.
  3. Stir in chili powder, cumin, garlic powder, salt, and pepper.
  4. Add diced tomatoes, tomato sauce, drained beans, and broth.
  5. Bring to a gentle simmer, then reduce heat.
  6. Cook uncovered for about 20–25 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  7. Taste and adjust seasonings. Serve with cornbread if you'd like!

Sweet Golden Cornbread

Soft, buttery, lightly sweet cornbread that's perfect with chili.

⏱ About 30 min 🍽 8 pieces

What you'll need ♡

  • 1 cup yellow cornmeal
  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1/3 cup granulated sugar
  • 1 tablespoon baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1 cup milk
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1/3 cup melted unsalted butter

Let's get cooking!

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F and grease an 8-inch square baking pan.
  2. Whisk cornmeal, flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt in a bowl.
  3. In a second bowl, whisk milk, eggs, and melted butter.
  4. Pour wet ingredients into dry ingredients and stir just until combined.
  5. Spread batter evenly in the prepared pan.
  6. Bake for about 20–25 minutes, until golden and a toothpick comes out clean.
  7. Let cool slightly, slice, and enjoy with a little extra butter.
